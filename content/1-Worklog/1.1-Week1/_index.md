### Week 1 Objectives:

Find team, get to know team members, and become familiar with the working environment.

Understand how to create, manage, and secure AWS accounts.

Use the AWS Console effectively: select the correct Region, search for services, and manage Favorites and the dashboard.

Understand and configure AWS Budgets to track costs, usage, and receive alerts.

Know how to create, manage, and handle support cases through AWS Support, including selecting the appropriate severity level and support plan.
