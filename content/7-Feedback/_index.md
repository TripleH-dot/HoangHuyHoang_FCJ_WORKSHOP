### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment is clean, airy, and positive. Everyone at FCJ is cheerful, friendly, and supportive, and they are always willing to answer questions and help us whenever we need it.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentors are always diligent and proactive, offering helpful advice to support everyone and help us correct our mistakes.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The program is a blend of learning and hands-on experience, which helps reduce confusion when entering real work later on. At the same time, through the program, we can see that cloud adoption is the future. Personally, I will apply the knowledge and experience gained here to future projects and my later career.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
During my time learning at FCJ, I gained not only cloud and technical knowledge but also soft skills. I had opportunities to work in teams, meet, and connect with senior members. From there, I can apply the knowledge, skills, and experience to my future path.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The culture mixes youthful openness with professionalism in work and behavior. Thanks to the seniors at FCJ, we learned the spirit of helping others — that giving is receiving — and growing together as a team.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The company provided us with a positive, friendly working environment and allowed us to get familiar with a professional workplace. Working hours were flexible so everyone could manage their schedules. Workshops were held regularly for us to learn and connect more.
